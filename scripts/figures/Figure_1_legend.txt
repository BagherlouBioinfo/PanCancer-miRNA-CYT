Figure 1. Analytical framework for recurrent miRNA–gene associations with tumour cytolytic activity. Discovery used 31 TCGA cancer types; primary CPE-adjusted testing included 20 eligible cancers. A pair entered the fixed set (47 pairs; 315 pair–cancer records) when all three arms met |ρ| ≥ 0.30 and BH-FDR < 0.05 within the same cancer in at least three cancers. The fixed set underwent sensitivity and specificity testing (LUMP, immune-population adjustment and matched non-target controls), pooled and exploratory analyses (random-effects meta-analysis, survival, clustering, mediation, conditional miR-146a/b, enrichment and drug annotation), and cellular-context assessment. These analyses did not redefine primary eligibility. Independent CPTAC breast-cancer assessment used the fixed pairs and thresholds without reselection. Arrows indicate workflow dependencies, not causal relationships. TCGA, The Cancer Genome Atlas; miRNA, microRNA; mRNA, messenger RNA; CYT, cytolytic activity; CPE, consensus purity estimate; BH, Benjamini–Hochberg; FDR, false discovery rate; LUMP, leukocyte unmethylation for purity; HC3, heteroskedasticity-consistent covariance estimator, type 3; CPTAC, Clinical Proteomic Tumor Analysis Consortium; WES, whole-exome sequencing.
